## 12. Avoid these (common small mistakes)

- ❌ Headline "Fresher" — recruiters filter for keywords, not "fresher". Use "Aspiring…" + skills.
- ❌ Sport/group photos.
- ❌ Sending mass "I'd like to connect" without a note on cold contacts.
- ❌ Listing a skill you can't speak 1 minute about in an interview.
- ✅ Keep every date accurate — recruiters check.
